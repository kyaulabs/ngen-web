Remove the switch, preference persistence, light CSS, and light logo variants from the active page; ignore old saved light preferences and the OS color scheme. Keep the supplied N upright; animate the surrounding geometry rather than the wordmark. The user approved the revised organic nebula and requested a transparent interior for the N’s central rounded square; retain its outline. Use the supplied dark/light schematics as the progressive-enhancement fallback. Honor reduced motion, pause while hidden, and offer a pause control.
- Verify 390×844 mobile, 1920×1080, 3840×2160, and 3840×2400 desktops in dark mode, plus 320px reflow. Also verify that a light OS preference and an old saved light preference cannot change the site’s dark appearance. `visual_review.json` covers mobile, 1080p, reflow, offline, dialogs, the scrolled dashboard, and the footer; browser tests cover the two larger desktop sizes.

Quality targets: semantic HTML, WCAG 2.2 AA, visible keyboard focus, contained dialog focus, progressive enhancement, and responsive content without horizontal page overflow. Primary touch controls target 44×44 CSS pixels, stricter than AA’s 24×24 minimum with exceptions. Reduced-motion support is an additional motion-safety measure. Browser evidence is local, controlled test content—not a claim of full accessibility conformance or user visual approval.
